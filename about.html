From roast to ritual

Our Story

ASADOR COFFEE was born from the shared belief that a coffee bar can feel like a writer's studio. We roast small lots, steep in conversation, and pour with an attention that slows the room.

Finding our flame

What started as weekend cupping sessions in a tiny garage became a full sensory workshop. We studied cacao notes, coaxed out plum sweetness, and restored a vintage Probat that still hums in our roastery.

Every roast is logged by hand, every blend tested with friends who never hesitate to give honest feedback. That ritual keeps us grounded and curious.

The heart of the space

Walnut tables, curated vinyl, and handwritten tasting notes invite guests to linger. We partner with local ceramicists, illustrators, and pastry makers so the cafe always feels alive with collaboration.

Our team greets regulars by name, saves favorite seats, and celebrates milestones with pour-overs on the house. Hospitality here is personal, never scripted.

Connected to community

We mentor young baristas, host open-mic nights for poets, and donate a portion of every seasonal release to neighborhood food initiatives. Coffee is our craft, but community is the reason we show up.

Whether you're drafting a novel, catching up with friends, or simply enjoying a quiet espresso, ASADOR is designed to feel like a second living room.
